Chapter 1 — The crossing
Sam set his sword against the parapet. His voice was low and measured.
"The bridge will hold," Sam said.
Mira, the engineer, shook her head. Her voice was bright and brisk.
"You said that about the last bridge," Mira replied.
"I was younger then," Sam said.
"It was yesterday," Mira said.
The wind rattled the ropes.
"Did you hear that?"
Nobody answered. It was impossible to tell which traveler had spoken.

Chapter 2 — The watch
Mira checked the ropes while Sam watched the road.
"Hold this steady," Mira said.
Sam gripped the cable.
"I have it," Sam replied.
"This is going to work," Mira thought.
A bell sounded beyond the river.

Chapter 72 — Home again
Sam, the swordsman who had crossed the bridge with Mira, returned at dawn.
"I told you I would come back," the swordsman said.
Mira put down her tools.
"And I told you to bring breakfast," Mira replied.
Sam laughed.
"Some things never change," Sam said.
